Merge Sort — CGPA Leaderboard

Max-Heap — Helpdesk Priority Queue
Array-based max-heap: parent priority >= children. heap[0] always holds highest priority. Left child of heap[i] = heap[2i+1] · Right = heap[2i+2]. Insert: siftUp O(log n) · Extract: siftDown O(log n).
